# Credits

This project is a single-screen variant of the Pokémon HeartGold/SoulSilver
decompilation. It is built on the work of everyone who contributed to that
decompilation; their commits are preserved in this repository's history
(`git shortlog -sn`).

- pret/pokeheartgold, the original decompilation project: https://github.com/pret/pokeheartgold
- ctrl-cheeb-del/pokeheartgold, the fork this work branched from: https://github.com/ctrl-cheeb-del/pokeheartgold
- The ssrecomp frontend that runs the build: https://github.com/ClickingMaster/ssrecomp

Pokémon and its games are trademarks of Nintendo, Creature Inc. and
GAME FREAK inc. No game assets are included: supply your own legally obtained
ROM to build.
